How to create a payslip

  1. Enter your company and employee details and (optionally) upload a logo.
  2. Set the pay period, then add earnings and deductions — the payslip totals update instantly.
  3. Pick a template and download a PDF or editable Word file, or print it.

Coming from the Cyprus salary calculator? Calculate your net pay first, then hit “Generate payslip” to carry the gross, income tax, Social Insurance and GESY straight into a ready-made Cyprus payslip.

Is it free and private?

Yes. There is no account and no cost. The generator runs entirely in your browser, so your payslip details never leave your device.

Can I download PDF and Word?

Yes — a print-ready A4 PDF and an editable Word (.docx) document, plus direct printing.

Which countries does it support?

Any country: earnings and deductions are fully custom. Cyprus figures can be auto-filled from the salary calculator; for other countries just type the amounts.
