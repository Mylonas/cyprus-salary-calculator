Cyprus salary & tax guides

Short, plain-English explainers for the 2026 rules behind your pay slip.

Open the calculator →

A Cyprus payslip has three main deductions between your gross and your net pay: income tax, Social Insurance and GESY. These guides explain each one for 2026 — how the tax bands work after the reform, what the contributions fund and where they cap, how the 13th and 14th salary are taxed, and how the picture changes if you're self-employed. Read them alongside the calculator to see exactly how your own number is built up.
