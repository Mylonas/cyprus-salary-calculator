Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026.

Your figures stay in your browser

Everything you type into the calculator — your salary and options — is processed entirely in your browser to work out the result. These figures are never sent to us or stored on any server. Refreshing the page clears them.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and do not ask for personal information to use the calculator. Like most websites, we may collect standard, non-identifying technical data through analytics and advertising partners — such as approximate region, browser and device type, and which pages are visited — to understand usage and keep the site running.

Cookies and advertising

To keep the calculator free, this site uses Google AdSense to show advertising. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. See the full Cookie Policy for details.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent notice, and personalised-advertising cookies are set only with consent, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

Local storage

The site may store a small preference such as your chosen language (EN/EL) in your browser. This never leaves your device.

Not financial advice

This calculator provides estimates for general guidance using published 2026 rates. It is not tax, legal or financial advice. Always verify with the Cyprus Tax Department, the Social Insurance Services, or a qualified accountant before making decisions.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes are reflected by the date above.
