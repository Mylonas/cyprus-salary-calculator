How the 13th salary is taxed

The 13th (and 14th) salary is part of your annual gross and is taxed exactly like the rest of your pay — there is no separate bonus-tax rate in Cyprus. Income tax is progressive and worked out on your total yearly income, so the extra month is taxed at your marginal band, with Social Insurance (8.8%) and GESY (2.65%) applied too, subject to their annual caps. This calculator computes the annual totals and divides the net across your chosen number of payments, so the "13th salary, net" figure is one payment's worth of take-home.

12, 13 or 14 — what actually changes

If your monthly gross is fixed, adding a 13th or 14th raises your annual total, so you owe more tax overall but keep more in absolute terms. What is the 13th salary?

The 13th salary is an extra month's pay that many employees in Cyprus receive, usually in December. It is not a statutory right for every worker — whether you are entitled to it depends on your individual employment contract or the collective agreement that covers your sector. Some contracts also provide for a 14th salary, typically paid before summer. The 13th and 14th salaries are part of your total annual compensation and are fully taxable — there is no special tax exemption for them.

Who gets a 13th salary?

Eligibility is determined by your contract, not by law. In the private sector, many companies include a 13th salary in the employment terms, especially in banking, insurance, large retail chains and multinational firms. In the semi-government sector and some public-sector bodies, the 13th salary is established by collective agreements. If your contract does not mention it, you are not entitled to it — always check your contract or ask your HR department.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 13th salary taxed differently?

No. The 13th and 14th salary are part of your annual gross and taxed at your marginal rate, along with Social Insurance and GESY. There is no separate bonus-tax rate in Cyprus.

Does getting 13 salaries mean more money overall?

Only if your monthly gross stays the same. If your contract states a monthly gross of €2,000 paid over 13 months, your annual gross is €26,000 — more than €24,000 (12 × €2,000). But some contracts simply divide the annual total into 13 payments, so each cheque is smaller. Check whether your stated salary is monthly or annual.

When is the 13th salary usually paid?

Typically in December, along with or just before the December salary. The 14th salary, where offered, is often paid in June or July, before the summer holidays.

Can my employer stop paying the 13th salary?

If the 13th salary is a term of your employment contract or collective agreement, your employer cannot unilaterally remove it without your consent. Any change to contractual terms requires agreement between both parties.
