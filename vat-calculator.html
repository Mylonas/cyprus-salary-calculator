Cyprus VAT rates in 2026

RateApplies to (examples)
19%Standard rate — most goods and services
9%Reduced — hotels, restaurants, catering, domestic passenger transport
5%Reduced — foodstuffs, medicines, books, some first-home supplies
0%Zero-rated — exports, some international services

These categories are examples only — the exact rate for a specific product or service is set by law and can change. Some supplies are exempt (for example certain financial, medical and rental services), which is not the same as zero-rated.

How the maths works

To add VAT, multiply the net amount by (1 + rate). At 19% that's net × 1.19. To remove VAT from a gross amount, divide by the same factor: net = gross ÷ 1.19, and the VAT is the difference. This calculator does both directions for any rate you pick.

When do you have to register for VAT?

A business must register for Cyprus VAT once taxable turnover passes the registration threshold (currently €15,600 in a 12-month period), and in some cross-border cases from the first euro.
