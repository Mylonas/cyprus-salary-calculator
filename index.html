This calculator works out your net take-home pay in Cyprus after all three mandatory deductions: income tax (progressive bands from 0% to 35%), Social Insurance (8.8% of gross, capped at €68,904 of insurable earnings) and the General Healthcare System contribution known as GESY (2.65%, capped at €180,000). It uses the 2026 post-reform rates, which raised the tax-free threshold from €19,500 to €22,000.

The calculator is designed for anyone working in Cyprus — whether you are a Cypriot employee, an expat considering a job offer, or an employer estimating total cost. It handles 12, 13 or 14 salary payments, pension/provident fund deductions, the 20% and 50% expat tax reliefs, and lets you compare two offers side by side. How the 2026 Cyprus salary calculation works

Your income tax is calculated on your gross salary after deducting Social Insurance, GESY and any pension contributions (together capped at 1/5 of gross, with pension counting up to 10% of pay) and any trade union dues (deductible in full). The first €22,000 of taxable income is tax-free following the January 2026 reform. The remaining income is taxed progressively: 20% from €22,001 to €32,000, 25% up to €42,000, 30% up to €72,000, and 35% above that. Social Insurance is 8.8% of gross (capped at €68,904 of insurable earnings) and the General Healthcare System (GESY/ΓεΣΥ) contribution is 2.65% (capped at €180,000).

What is the tax-free amount in Cyprus for 2026?

After the 2026 reform, the personal income tax-free threshold rose from €19,500 to €22,000 per year.

What is deducted from a Cyprus salary?

Employees pay income tax, Social Insurance (8.8%) and GESY (2.65%). Employers pay their own Social Insurance and GESY plus contributions to other state funds.

Does this include the 13th salary?

Yes — choose 12, 13 or 14 salaries. The annual gross is taxed as a whole and then divided across the number of payments you select.

What gross salary do I need to take home a certain amount?

Use the Reverse tab: enter the net take-home you want per month or per year and the calculator works out the gross salary you'd need to sign for.

What is the 50% expat tax relief?

Individuals taking up first employment in Cyprus with pay above €55,000 per year can exempt 50% of that employment income from income tax for up to 17 years. Tick "50% expat relief" under Advanced options to estimate it.
